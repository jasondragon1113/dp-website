What age can kids start?

From age 3 with ESM, then SPM at 6 and VEX GO at 7 — and it keeps going all the way through high school.

How big are the classes?

Small classes — 10 kids max.

Do we need to buy a kit?

No. Every child gets their own kit in class — no sharing needed.

Can we make up a missed class?

Yes. Just let us know and we'll set up a make-up class.

What if my child wants to compete?

Yes — we have Competition Teams for VEX, FIRST and WRO. VEX IQ / V5 teams start at the end of June each year.See Competition Team
