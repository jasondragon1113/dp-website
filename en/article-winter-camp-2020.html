Class News2019/12/11

Winter Camps Are Coming to the Robotics Lab!

This article is from 2019; some details may have changed.

The moment you've all been waiting for!
The winter camps you've missed so much are finally here! 💞💞
If you love building things with your hands, come learn robotics with us!
With the experienced teachers at Dr. Player Robotic Lab as your guides, learn mechanisms and programming in a fun, brain-teasing way that keeps up with Taiwan's new 108 Curriculum Guidelines. Come learn CODING with us!

In addition to our regular camps, this year we're adding the popular “Arduino Self-Driving Car” camp! You'll create your own car from nothing, which feels a lot like being a maker!

🌍VEX IQ Robotics Camp (Ages 10–14)

New this year from the US, the VEX IQ robot series is the designated platform for the WorldSkills Juniors category, and it uses ROBOTC, which emulates the C language, so students can go further in learning advanced programming skills!
This is a full-day course, lunch included. Bringing your own laptop is recommended.

🔫Pneumatic Blaster Course (Ages 10 and Up)

Upgrade your making skills! A maker course that combines software and hardware, from joining pipes to 3D printing. On the last day you can even play a survival game with the blaster you made yourself!
Lunch included. Bringing your own laptop is recommended. You can take the blaster you build in class home with you!

🏅Minecraft (Ages 9 and Up)

Minecraft is one of the most popular games in the world right now, loved by adults and kids alike. Microsoft has even released an education edition, letting students create hands-on and solve problems, putting into practice our philosophy of learning through “play.”
Lunch included, plus one Minecraft Education Edition account. Bringing your own laptop is recommended.

🤖DIY Arduino Car (Grade 7 and Up)

Design from scratch and build a car from nothing, learning to integrate software and hardware along the way and picking up lots of maker skills!

(This session of camps has ended. For the latest camps, see Holiday Camps.)

What age can kids start?

From age 3 with ESM, then SPM at 6 and VEX GO at 7 — and it keeps going all the way through high school.

How big are the classes?

Small classes — 10 kids max.

Do we need to buy a kit?

No. Every child gets their own kit in class — no sharing needed.

Can we make up a missed class?

Yes. Just let us know and we'll set up a make-up class.

What if my child wants to compete?

Yes — we have Competition Teams for VEX, FIRST and WRO. VEX IQ / V5 teams start at the end of June each year.See Competition Team
