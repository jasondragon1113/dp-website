Screw It off the field

Beyond competing, FTC 11047 has toured around Taiwan, built aircraft and brought Taiwanese culture to the World Championship.

2023/07/03

Jiutian Cup Third Prince Extreme Taiwan Relay

At the 11th Jiutian Cup, Screw It ran alongside the Techno Third Prince (Nezha) on the stretch from Daya, Taichung to Xiushui, Changhua.

2023/04/27

Bringing the Techno Third Prince to the World Championship

We borrowed the Techno Third Prince from the Jiutian Folk Art Troupe as our mascot. It made us one of the most popular teams at the World Championship in Houston, and we were featured many times on FIRST's official Instagram.

2022/09/18

Red Bull Flugtag

In the off-season, we took on building our own flying machine. Our design was picked from more than 600 teams, flew for real at Taichung Port, and won 2nd place for best landing.

What age can kids start?

From age 3 with ESM, then SPM at 6 and VEX GO at 7 — and it keeps going all the way through high school.

How big are the classes?

Small classes — 10 kids max.

Do we need to buy a kit?

No. Every child gets their own kit in class — no sharing needed.

Can we make up a missed class?

Yes. Just let us know and we'll set up a make-up class.

What if my child wants to compete?

Yes — we have Competition Teams for VEX, FIRST and WRO. VEX IQ / V5 teams start at the end of June each year.See Competition Team
