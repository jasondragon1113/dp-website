A teacher's story

From Manzhou, Pingtung to Taipei Da'an

Teacher Peng Peng, a sixth-cohort Teach For Taiwan fellow, spent four years at Changle Elementary in Manzhou, Pingtung — teaching the school's first-ever computer class, starting a robotics club, leading the kids to a national engineering creativity title, and taking on FLL. Since coming back to Taipei to open Dr. Player Da'an, Teacher Peng Peng has also been training school teachers in robotics in Hsinchu and beyond.

“You don't have to be great to start, but you have to start to be great.”

Teacher Peng Peng

What age can kids start?

From age 3 with ESM, then SPM at 6 and VEX GO at 7 — and it keeps going all the way through high school.

How big are the classes?

Small classes — 10 kids max.

Do we need to buy a kit?

No. Every child gets their own kit in class — no sharing needed.

Can we make up a missed class?

Yes. Just let us know and we'll set up a make-up class.

What if my child wants to compete?

Yes — we have Competition Teams for VEX, FIRST and WRO. VEX IQ / V5 teams start at the end of June each year.See Competition Team
