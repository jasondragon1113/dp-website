Congratulations! A Team Coached by Dr. Player Robotic Lab Wins 2nd Place in the World at WorldSkills Juniors

This article is from 2019; some details may have changed.

[International Competition News]
Good news from our competitors at the world final in Russia!
Coached by the Dr. Player Robotic Lab team,
Chiayi Industrial Vocational High School: students Lin and Shen
earned, at WORLDSKILLS JUNIORS,

2nd place in the world in the Robotics category 🥈🥈🥈
Simply amazing!! 🎉Congratulations🎉
For the past few months the competitors have practically lived in Taichung,
at Dr. Player Robotic Lab's national team training center,
training day and night!
And after all those months, they finally made it onto the world stage.
We are absolutely thrilled 💪
Thank you to the chief judge and the professors,
to the department head and teachers at Chiayi Industrial Vocational High School,
and to all the partners who supported the competitors along the way!

What age can kids start?

From age 3 with ESM, then SPM at 6 and VEX GO at 7 — and it keeps going all the way through high school.

How big are the classes?

Small classes — 10 kids max.

Do we need to buy a kit?

No. Every child gets their own kit in class — no sharing needed.

Can we make up a missed class?

Yes. Just let us know and we'll set up a make-up class.

What if my child wants to compete?

Yes — we have Competition Teams for VEX, FIRST and WRO. VEX IQ / V5 teams start at the end of June each year.See Competition Team
